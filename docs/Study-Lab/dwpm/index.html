Vorbereitung auf die vorgezogene Nachschreibeklausur

Dein Einstieg

Lernpfad zur Nachschreibeklausur öffnen: Diagnose, Reihenfolge, Rechentraining und vollständige Probeklausur. Erklärungen auf Deutsch, Klausurantworten auf Englisch.

Der vorhandene Trainer ist nutzbar, aber der vollständige Folien-/Grafikaudit ist noch offen. Ein als „secure“ markiertes Item ist eine Selbsteinschätzung, kein bestandener Leistungstest.

What This Is

This is a Quarto prototype for turning the Digital Work and Project Management material into a focused exam-prep website.

The exam strategy is simple:

  1. Learn the exam frame and answer style first.
  2. Secure the Project Management 60 points with compact templates.
  3. Secure the Digital Work 60 points through frameworks and case application.
  4. Practice in English under time pressure.
Important

The exam material says: answers must be written in English. German answers are not graded in the mock exam.

Source-based task training

Open the answer-first task trainer: 43 training items from Mock Exam, DW1, DW2 and PM recaps (Mock: 120 points), original sources, methods, pitfalls, local progress and a searchable page register. First usable batch, not complete exercise coverage.

Exam Frame

  • 120 minutes.
  • 120 points.
  • 60 points Project Management.
  • 60 points Digital Work.
  • Roughly one point per minute.
  • Use compact English answers: define, explain, apply, evaluate.

Universal Answer Template

  1. Define the concept.
  2. Explain the mechanism.
  3. Apply it to concrete case evidence.
  4. Name one risk or limitation.
  5. Give a justified recommendation or conclusion.

Learning Loop

Use this loop for every study session:

  1. Pick one block from the material map.
  2. Read the core note for 15 minutes.
  3. Answer two practice questions without looking.
  4. Compare against the expected point structure.
  5. Convert every missing point into a flashcard or answer fragment.

Site Direction

The target shape is an Obsidian-style study web: every lecture block becomes a linked page, every high-yield concept becomes a wiki node, and every node gets a small self-check before it counts as learned.
